About

Idol Ridge Winery & Alder Creek Distillery

Idol Ridge Winery is one of the most welcoming and visually stunning stops on the east shore of Seneca Lake — a lodge-style tasting room perched up on a ridge in Lodi with panoramic views of the lake stretching out below. Opened in November 2013 by the Martin family — owners of Montezuma Winery and Hidden Marsh Distillery in Seneca Falls — Idol Ridge brings the same warmth and creative spirit to the Finger Lakes wine trail that has defined the Martin brand for decades.

The wine program is extensive and adventurous — more than 20 different wines spanning dry to sweet, traditional vinifera, sparkling, fruit and honey wines under the Fossenvue brand, and the newer wild-fermented Local Culture series. Head Winemaker Phil Plummer and Assistant Winemaker Alex Bienvenue keep the cellar exciting, producing award winners like the WE 90-point Blanc de Noir and the Best in Sparkling Vinifera Sparkling Riesling String of Pearls alongside funky naturals like Pinot Noir Pét-Nat and Ramato.

Alder Creek Distillery runs alongside the winery, producing premium spirits including the signature Smoked Bourbon Whiskey and Best of Class Nashi Pear Brandy. The tasting room also features homemade fudge, local bites, a gift shop, and private Airbnb cabins for overnight stays on the ridge.

In Our Words

Our Story

Pull up the driveway at Idol Ridge and you immediately understand why people keep coming back. The stone sign, the rolling green grounds, the lodge sitting up on the ridge — it all feels like arriving somewhere special. Open the door and the fireplace, the warm wood tones, and the smell of homemade fudge make it impossible not to settle in for a while.

The wine list is genuinely fun — there's something for everyone from dry Riesling to sparkling Saperavi to a Pét-Nat if you want to get adventurous. Add in the Alder Creek spirits tasting and you've got one of the most complete tasting experiences on the east shore. And on a Sunday afternoon with live music on the deck and Seneca Lake laid out in front of you, it's about as good as the Finger Lakes gets.

— South East Seneca

Tips for Visitors

  • Try the Sparkling Saperavi — it's one of the most unique sparkling wines you'll find anywhere on the Seneca Lake trail.
  • Add a spirits tasting — the Nashi Pear Brandy won Best of Class and it's genuinely exceptional.
  • Don't leave without trying the homemade fudge — it's a signature of the Martin family tasting rooms.
  • Sunday live music runs 1:30–4:30 PM from Memorial Day through August — plan around it if you can.
  • Groups of 12 or more require a reservation — call (607) 582-7773 ahead of your visit.
  • Dogs are welcome on the grounds — one of the more pet-friendly stops on the north end of the trail.
  • Private Airbnb cabins are available for overnight stays — book through their website or Airbnb directly.
